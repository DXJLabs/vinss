## 2. Privacy Design Principle

VINSS is designed to reduce unnecessary exposure of private commercial coordination.

Our intended privacy principle is:

```text
collect less
→ expose less
→ retain less
→ disclose selectively
```

However:

```text
encrypted
        ≠
anonymous

private coordination
        ≠
no metadata

blockchain
        ≠
erasable database

self-custody wallet
        ≠
no personal data
```

Some VINSS information remains public or can become linked to a person depending on context.

## 6. Encrypted Data and Commitments

VINSS may use encrypted payloads, ciphertext chunks, commitments, and opaque routing values.

Encryption can reduce exposure of plaintext content.

Encryption does not prevent all processing of:

- transaction metadata;
- wallet addresses;
- timing;
- contract interaction;
- ciphertext size;
- routing identifiers;
- public settlement state;
- network-level data.

A cryptographic commitment may show that particular data was committed or referenced.

It does not automatically prove that the underlying content is true.
