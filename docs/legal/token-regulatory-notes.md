# VINSS Token Regulatory Notes

> **Purpose:** keep future VINSS token, Points, Settlement Certificate/SBT, presale, staking/locking, and VINSS → DXJ concepts legally separated from the current VINSS product until a final token design is frozen and reviewed.

**Status:** Future-design issue spotting only
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-30
**Owner:** DXJ Labs
**Product:** VINSS

> **Important:** This document is not legal advice, a token legal opinion, an offering document, a white paper, a promise to issue VINSS, or a statement that any future VINSS token sale, distribution, reward, staking, conversion, or secondary-market activity is lawful in any jurisdiction.

---

# 1. Token Is Not Required for the Current Product

VINSS can operate as a transaction-fee product without a VINSS token.

Keep the current product economically and legally separate from future token design.

```text
VINSS product
        ≠
Points
        ≠
Settlement Certificate / SBT
        ≠
VINSS token
        ≠
VINSS presale
        ≠
VINSS → DXJ mechanism
```

Likewise:

```text
product fee revenue
        ≠
token-sale proceeds
```

and:

```text
mainnet launch
        ≠
token launch.
```

Do not create a token merely because:

```text
VINSS is on mainnet;
Points exist;
community members request a token;
a hackathon ends;
competitors launch tokens;
a token could fund operations.
```

# 19. United States — Current 2026 SEC Baseline

On 17 March 2026, the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission issued an interpretive release titled:

```text
Application of the Federal Securities Laws to Certain Types
of Crypto Assets and Certain Transactions Involving Crypto Assets
```

Effective date:

```text
23 March 2026
```

Official source:

https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2026/03/s7-2026-09

The interpretation provides a token taxonomy and addresses how certain non-security crypto assets can become subject to, and later cease to be subject to, an investment contract.

This 2026 interpretation is more relevant to a future U.S. VINSS analysis than relying only on older generic crypto commentary.

```text
Digital Commodities
```

# 27. MiCA White Paper


# 28. MiCA Future-Value Claims

Article 6 of MiCA states that the relevant crypto-asset white paper shall not contain assertions regarding the future value of the crypto-asset except for the specific required statement described by the Regulation.

This is directly relevant to token communications.


# 76. Core Token Principle

VINSS should not create legal and economic promises before they are necessary.

Preferred sequence:

```text
build useful product
        ↓
measure real usage
        ↓
prove unit economics
        ↓
define token necessity
        ↓
freeze token rights
        ↓
stress-test economics
        ↓
complete jurisdiction analysis
        ↓
prepare sale/distribution controls
        ↓
launch only if justified.
```

The core rule is:

> **Points are not a token, tokenomics are not a promise, utility labels are not legal conclusions, and no VINSS or VINSS → DXJ economic right should become public or binding until the exact design is technically, economically, and legally ready.**
